A small used computer can be a smart buy for the front desk, a shared office PC, or a first try at running AI on your own machine. Here's how to buy one without regret.

If you're buying for local AI, memory matters most. We covered that in [what fits on a 64 GB Mac mini](https://morgangrok.github.io/backofficecrew/posts/slot2-ally-mac-mini/) and [what a 2022 Mac Studio can run](https://morgangrok.github.io/backofficecrew/posts/mac-studio-local-ai/). This post is about the buying part.

## Before you buy: read the listing like a skeptic

**1. The return window.** Find it before you look at anything else. The FTC says some marketplaces leave returns and refunds entirely up to the seller, and even when a site helps, "there may be a short window of time" to return the item or claim a refund ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-online-marketplace)). No returns? Think hard before you buy.

**2. Specs as listed vs. specs as shown.** The FTC suggests looking closely at the photos. If all you see are stock photos, you can't check the item's condition or be sure the seller has it ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-online-marketplace)). Ask for a photo of the actual machine's spec screen. Compare it with what the listing says.

**3. Memory and storage.** Write down the exact amounts the listing promises. You'll check them on arrival. For Windows 11, Microsoft's minimum is 4 GB of memory and 64 GB of storage ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/compatibility/windows-11-system-requirements)). That's a floor, not a goal.

**4. Ports.** Count the ports you need: monitors, keyboard, network cable, USB drives. Then count them in the photos.

**5. The power adapter.** Ask if it's in the box. If the model uses a separate power brick, ask if it's the original one.

**6. The operating system and its license.** Microsoft stopped security updates for Windows 10 on October 14, 2025 ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/deployment/updates-lifecycle/windows-10-support-has-ended-on-october-14-2025)). Windows 11 needs, among other things, a TPM 2.0 chip and a processor on Microsoft's approved list ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/compatibility/windows-11-system-requirements)). Ask which version it runs and whether it's activated. If you buy a refurbished PC, Microsoft says the refurbisher, not Microsoft, must give you a product key ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/activation/activate-windows)).

**7. The account lock (Macs).** On recent Macs, turning on Find My turns on Activation Lock. Then nobody can erase the Mac or set it up again without the owner's Apple Account password or passcode ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102541)). Ask the seller to sign out and remove it from their account before it ships.

**8. Warranty transfer.** Some makers move leftover warranty to a new owner, but you have to ask. Dell, for example, wants the previous owner's details plus the Service Tag and Express Code, and says a domestic transfer takes 7 to 10 business days ([Dell](https://www.dell.com/support/kbdoc/en-us/000125103/ownership-transfer-and-dell-product-registration-guides)). For a Mac, ask for the serial number and enter it at Apple's [checkcoverage.apple.com](https://checkcoverage.apple.com/) ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102767)).

Want the least guesswork? Apple Certified Refurbished Macs come with Apple's one-year limited warranty, plus free shipping and returns ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/shop/refurbished/about)).

## Red flags: walk away

- **Odd payment requests.** The FTC says don't pay by wire transfer, gift cards, reload cards or crypto, and don't buy from anyone who wants payment outside the marketplace. Use a credit card or a secure payment system that protects buyers ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-online-marketplace)).
- **A price that's too good.** A very low price on an expensive item may mean it's fake ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-online-marketplace)).
- **"As is."** The FTC notes that words like "used," "refurbished" or "as is" mean it probably isn't in top condition ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-online-marketplace)). Price it that way.
- **A Mac still on someone's account.** If the seller can't remove Activation Lock, you'll need proof of purchase to ask Apple for help ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102541)).
- **Vague answers.** Get answers in writing and keep copies of your messages ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-online-marketplace)).

## The day it arrives: test before the window closes

Put the return deadline on your calendar first. The FTC says to keep track of it ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-online-marketplace)). Then:

1. **Check the specs.** On Windows, open Settings, then System, then About. It shows the processor and the amount of memory installed. Storage is under System, then Storage ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/find-information-about-your-windows-device)). On a Mac, open System Settings, then General, then About. Click Details for warranty coverage, or System Report for the installed hardware ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/get-system-information-about-your-mac-syspr35536/mac)).
2. **Check activation.** On Windows 11, go to Settings, then System, then Activation ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/activation/activate-windows)).
3. **Run the built-in hardware test on a Mac.** Apple Diagnostics helps find which part might be at fault. On Apple silicon, hold the power button until you see Options, then hold Command-D ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102550)).
4. **Plug everything in.** Every port, your monitor, your network cable. Use the power adapter that came with it.
5. **Start fresh.** Don't trust the last owner's setup. On Windows, use Reset this PC with "Remove everything" ([Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/backup-recovery/reset-your-pc)). On a Mac, use Erase All Content and Settings. It needs macOS Monterey or later and an Apple silicon or T2 Mac ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102664)). Then confirm it still activates.
6. **Start the warranty transfer** if the maker offers one.

If anything doesn't match the listing, message the seller that day, in writing. Buy it like you might need to send it back.
